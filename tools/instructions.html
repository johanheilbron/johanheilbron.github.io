Your website: how it works

Now at johanheilbron.github.io; later at johanheilbron.nl.

1. Your to-do list for the final version

  1. A photo. Choose a portrait and copy it into the folder Documenten › website › images. Any file name is fine.
  2. The About text. Write a new version in Word or in an e-mail, whichever is easiest. It will be put in place for you.
  3. The PDFs of your publications. Put them in the right folders; see section 2.
  4. The design. Look at the website and note what you would like to change.

2. Adding PDFs

  1. Open File Explorer (the yellow folder icon in the taskbar, or press Windows key + E).
  2. Go to Documenten › website › bib. There you find five folders, one for each section of your publication list:
booksbooks, edited volumes, brochures
scientific-editionsthe Bourdieu and Elias editions
articles-chaptersarticles, book chapters, reviews, essays
journal-issuesthematic journal issues
filmthe film
  1. Copy or drag each PDF into the matching folder.
  • File names do not matter. You do not need to rename anything.
  • Not sure which folder? Pick the closest one; a wrong folder is not a problem.
  • Keep each PDF under about 20 MB if you can (large scans can be made smaller).
  • The PDFs appear on the website only after the next synchronisation (section 4).

3. Your Word files remain the source

The website takes your publication list and your CV from your own Word files:

  • - 1 PUBL78NU(NL).docx → the Publications page
  • - 2 Heilbron CV (English complete) 2026.docx → the CV page

Keep working in them exactly as before. Nothing on the website ever changes these files. Typos in them also appear on the website, so corrections are best made there.

4. Putting changes online

After you have added PDFs or changed your Word files:

  1. Open the Claude app, go to Code, and choose the folder website.
  2. Type: Sync the website.

Claude reads your Word files, links each new PDF to the right publication (it gets a Download link), puts everything online and tells you what it changed. If it cannot identify a PDF with certainty, it tells you so and asks; it never guesses. The website is updated about two minutes later.

Please do not move or rename the folders inside website, and leave the other files there alone. Questions: ask Micha, or ask Claude in the website folder.
