## Current positions

- 2018–present: Directeur de recherche émérite, CNRS, Centre européen de sociologie et de science politique (CESSP), Paris
- 2018–present: Emeritus, Erasmus Center for Economic Sociology, Erasmus University Rotterdam

## Education

- 1972–1979: Philosophy and sociology, University of Amsterdam (doctoraal in sociology, cum laude)
- 1979–1980: Sociology, philosophy and history, Paris; research seminars at the École des hautes études en sciences sociales
- PhD in political and social sciences, University of Amsterdam, cum laude (supervisor: Johan Goudsblom)

## Positions

- 2024–2025: Visiting professor, King Willem-Alexander Chair for Dutch Studies, Université de Liège
- 2019–2023: Guest professor, Department of Education, Uppsala University
- 2008–2018: Directeur de recherche, CNRS, CESSP, Paris
- 2008–2018: Professor of economic sociology and founding director, Erasmus Center for Economic Sociology, Erasmus University Rotterdam
- 2004–2008: Norbert Elias chair, Erasmus University Rotterdam
- 2000–2008: Chargé de recherche, CNRS, CESSP, Paris
- 2000–2004: Norbert Elias chair, Faculty of Social Sciences, Utrecht University
- 1996–2000: Chargé de recherche, CNRS, CLERSE, Lille
- 1995–1996: Lecturer, Institute of Sociology, University of Amsterdam
- 1990–1992: Researcher, Department of Art and Culture Studies, Erasmus University Rotterdam
- 1990: Associate maître de conférences, École des hautes études en sciences sociales, Paris
- 1986–1987: Lecturer, Department of Sociology and History, University of Amsterdam
- 1983–1985: Fellow of the Fondation Hugot du Collège de France and the Maison des sciences de l'homme, Centre de sociologie européenne, Paris
- 1981–1982: Researcher, SISWO, Amsterdam

## Fellowships

- 2017–2018: Member, School of Social Science, Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton
- 2015: Fellow, CIRHUS, New York University
